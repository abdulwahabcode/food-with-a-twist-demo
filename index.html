Our story

One home kitchen, a lot of love

Food with a twist began the way the best recipes do — at home. What started as cooking for family and friends in Tarneit grew into a little home kitchen with a devoted weekly following.

Every week we post a new menu: the Indian classics we grew up on, alongside fusion dishes we dream up in between. Everything is cooked in small batches, packed with care, and made to taste like someone's favourite person cooked it.

In 2026, our kitchen was honoured as 2026 Home Enterprise of the Year by the Williams Landing Business Association — a proud moment for a very small kitchen.
